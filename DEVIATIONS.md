# Deviations from the pre-registration

Every change or addition to `PREREGISTRATION.md` made after work began, with its date and reason. Clarifications fill in details the plan left open; additions are extra analyses reported alongside, never instead of, the registered ones. Entries marked **after results** were added after the corresponding results had been seen; every other entry was made before.

| Date | Section | Type | Change | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-10-06 | Data | clarification | AMFI NAV history starts in April 2006, so the first monthly return is May 2006. | The March 2006 request returns no data. |
| 2026-10-06 | Universe | clarification | Excluded as non-domestic or non-equity even when filed under an equity label: overseas and global funds, fund-of-funds and multi-manager products, commodity funds, hybrids (including equity savings), and index funds or ETFs filed under pre-2018 labels. | Old AMFI labels ("Growth") mix these with domestic equity funds; found while checking classifications, before any return statistic. |
| 2026-10-06 | Universe | clarification | Growth options are identified after removing fund-name phrases ("Dividend Yield", "Dividend Opportunities", "Growth & Value"), so a "Dividend Yield Fund - Growth" plan counts as growth. Institutional plans and bonus options are excluded. | Otherwise growth plans of dividend-yield funds were misread as dividend options. |
| 2026-10-06 | Data cleaning | clarification | A monthly NAV change above +100% or below -60% is treated as a data error and dropped; the count is reported in `results/tables/data_quality.csv`. | No equity fund's true monthly return is outside this band in the sample period; such values are recording errors. |
| 2026-10-06 | RQ2 | clarification | False-discovery-rate p-values follow Barras, Scaillet and Wermers (2010) exactly: Newey-West t-statistics and a per-fund residual bootstrap (1,000 draws). The 36-month minimum stays primary; their 60-month minimum is reported as a check. | The plan named the method but not its p-value step. |
| 2026-10-06 | RQ3 | clarification | The primary timing window starts in 2019-04, the first month of AMFI's consistent category layout. The 2018-04 to 2019-03 months use an older layout whose equity total is not comparable (equity AUM drops from Rs 8.05 to 7.04 lakh crore across the switch with a flat market). | "As far as the format allows" in the plan. |
| 2026-10-06 | RQ3 | addition | Besides the registered permutation test (flows shuffled across all months), a second test shuffles flows only within each calendar year, and a regression asks whether flows follow past returns and precede future ones. | Equity flows rose strongly over 2019-2026 (SIP growth); shuffling across years mixes that trend with timing. The within-year test isolates month-to-month timing. |
| 2026-10-06 | Exploratory | addition | Whether SEBI's 2017 categorisation made large-cap funds more index-like: difference-in-differences in tracking error against a Nifty 50 index fund, large-cap vs mid- and small-cap funds, before and after mid-2018. | Not pre-registered; reported as exploratory. |
| 2026-10-06 | Rupee estimates | clarification | The regular-plan share of active equity AUM is taken from AMFI's FY2025 annual report: direct plans held 12% of SIP AUM in March 2020 and 21% in March 2025. Applying the SIP split to all active equity AUM is labelled an assumption, with a sensitivity range. | No public source splits all equity AUM by plan. |
| 2026-10-06 | RQ2 | addition | Empirical-Bayes shrinkage of each fund's alpha (normal prior estimated from the cross-section), giving an expected alpha after luck and P(true alpha > 0); validated out of sample (estimate 2013-2019, realise 2020-2025, mean squared error and rank correlation, raw vs shrunk). Used in the scorecard. | Raw alphas are noisy; investors need an estimate that already discounts luck. Added before the full-data run. |
| 2026-10-06 | RQ2 | addition | Survivorship check: average alpha of funds alive at the end vs funds that closed earlier. | Most public Indian fund comparisons use today's fund list; this measures how much that flatters active management. |
| 2026-10-06 | RQ1 | addition | External anchor: total commissions AMFI reports were paid to top distributors in FY 2024-25 (its distributor-wise disclosure), and the implied commission rate on their AUM. | An independent check on the rupee size of the commission leak. |
| 2026-10-06 | RQ2 | addition | Calibration check: the four-factor alpha of each index-fund series (Nifty 50, Sensex, Next 50, Midcap 150, Smallcap 250, Nifty 500). Active funds' alphas are read against it. | A development run on partial data showed index funds themselves carrying non-zero four-factor alpha, which would mean part of any fund's "alpha" is factor-model mismatch. Added before the full-data run. |
| 2026-10-06 | Universe | correction | Direct-plan series start in 2013-01 in every analysis, as the plan already said. The code had not enforced it: Quantum Mutual Fund sold only direct plans before SEBI's 2013 rule and AMFI kept those codes as "Direct Plan", so one or two Quantum funds formed the whole "direct" universe for 2006-2012. Everything was rerun after the fix. | Found from an impossible number (a 10.5% "direct" alpha for 2006-2010, before direct plans existed). |
| 2026-10-06 | Exploratory | addition, **after results** | Decay check (`decay.py`): rolling 36-month alpha of the average fund; alpha in fixed sub-periods; the selection strategies split into 2014-2019 and 2020-2025; and the commission gap against alpha within categories. | The empirical-Bayes validation showed that funds' 2013-2019 alphas were followed by much smaller 2020-2025 alphas (mean 0.24% a year). Exploratory; no registered result changes. |
| 2026-10-06 | Exploratory | addition, **after results** | Within the decay check: each calendar year's abnormal return for the average direct plan (factor betas from the whole window), the alpha without 2020, and 2020-2025 alpha for funds with three or more years of history before 2020 against newer funds. | To see whether the weaker 2020-2025 alpha is one bad year, older funds fading, or a general loss of edge. Exploratory. |
| 2026-10-06 | Scorecard | correction | Fund names in the scorecard came out as the last word of each name ("Fund") because a multi-character split pattern is read as a regular expression by pandas. Fixed with a literal split; no number changed. | Found while preparing the write-up. |
